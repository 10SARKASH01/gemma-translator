# Android offline speech models

The APK uses the official **Sherpa-ONNX 1.13.8 Android AAR** for both speech recognition and synthesis. The app loads model files from its private model directory; inference never downloads a model and never sends audio or text to a service. Only the explicit Offline setup screen uses the network.

The pinned runtime is [sherpa-onnx-1.13.8.aar](https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/releases/download/v1.13.8/sherpa-onnx-1.13.8.aar), SHA-256 `633c24321e06b1fe79feafa03ea16cbc0f8a286641e2da3559bac91bdb13bd96`. Its Kotlin API is `com.k2fsa.sherpa.onnx`; the APK includes only its ARM64 native libraries.

## Language routing

| Language | Recognition | Neural speech output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arabic `ar` | Multilingual Whisper small INT8, forced `ar` | Supertonic 3 INT8, `lang=ar` |
| English `en` | Multilingual Whisper small INT8, forced `en` | Supertonic 3 INT8, `lang=en` |
| Spanish `es` | Multilingual Whisper small INT8, forced `es` | Supertonic 3 INT8, `lang=es` |
| Japanese `ja` | Multilingual Whisper small INT8, forced `ja` | Supertonic 3 INT8, `lang=ja` |
| Chinese `zh` | Multilingual Whisper small INT8, forced `zh` | Kokoro INT8, Chinese lexicon, Xiaoxiao speaker 47 |
| Korean `ko` | Multilingual Whisper small INT8, forced `ko` | Supertonic 3 INT8, `lang=ko` |
| Persian `fa` | Multilingual Whisper small INT8, forced `fa` | Piper `fa_IR-amir-medium`, Persian phonemizer |
| Urdu `ur` | Multilingual Whisper small INT8, forced `ur` | Piper `ur_PK-fasih-medium`, Urdu phonemizer |
| French `fr` | Multilingual Whisper small INT8, forced `fr` | Supertonic 3 INT8, `lang=fr` |

Whisper runs the **transcribe** task. Gemma performs translation separately. Switching source language updates the recognizer configuration without loading a second copy of its weights. Whisper's native decoder supports only greedy search in this runtime; accuracy can differ from the Pi's whisper.cpp beam decoding. Recordings longer than 28 seconds are divided into consecutive windows so native Whisper cannot silently truncate the recording. Words crossing a window boundary may be harder to recognize.

The app retains one speech recognition model and one voice engine. The six Supertonic languages share the same engine and use an explicit language parameter. Changing to Persian, Urdu, or Chinese releases the previous voice before loading the new one. Supertonic uses speaker 6 and eight synthesis steps. Piper uses its single trained speaker. Missing or incomplete models produce a setup error; unsupported languages never use an English voice.

## Download catalog

[SpeechAssets.kt](app/src/main/java/com/gemmatranslator/offline/SpeechAssets.kt) pins the download URLs, byte counts, SHA-256 hashes, and files required by every model. The five speech archives total approximately **1.03 GB** of downloads, separately from Gemma. The Whisper archive also includes unused full precision weights; setup discards these while retaining the INT8 encoder and decoder.

The Persian and Urdu voices use eSpeak NG **to obtain phonemes**; their final sound is generated by a neural Piper model. Android Urdu speech output therefore differs from the Pi's earlier direct eSpeak synthesis.

## Model and runtime notices

Downloaded archives retain their `LICENSE` and `MODEL_CARD` files alongside model files. The pinned artifacts were inspected during development:

- **Sherpa-ONNX:** [Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/blob/v1.13.8/LICENSE). Its native third-party dependencies have separate terms, including ONNX Runtime's MIT license and eSpeak NG's GPLv3-or-later license. [Sherpa eSpeak build source](https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/tree/v1.13.8/cmake) and [eSpeak NG source and license](https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng).
- **Whisper:** [MIT code/model license](https://github.com/openai/whisper/blob/main/LICENSE).
- **Supertonic 3:** The selected converted archive includes Supertone's MIT `LICENSE`; preserve that file. [Original model archive](https://huggingface.co/supertone-oss-archive/supertonic-3).
- **Kokoro:** The selected archive includes an Apache 2.0 `LICENSE`. [Creator's model card](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M).
- **Persian Amir:** The archive's model card specifies a CC0 dataset, `fa_IR`, and 22,050 Hz output. [Original voice model card](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/main/fa/fa_IR/amir/medium/MODEL_CARD).
- **Urdu Fasih:** The archive's model card specifies MIT, `ur_PK`, the Urdu `ur` phonemizer, and 22,050 Hz output. [Creator's voice repository](https://huggingface.co/IhorShevchuk/piper-voice-ur-fasih).

The integrated native eSpeak NG phonemizer means redistribution must satisfy its GPL source and notice requirements as well as the project's and models' licenses. Keep this project's Android source, pinned upstream speech source and build information available with redistributed APKs. This is a sideload implementation; store publication needs a review of the complete distribution's signing and license notices.

## Verification performed

Five JVM tests cover every language's forced transcription code, explicit neural voice selection, unsupported-language rejection, actionable missing-model errors, pinned catalog hashes, shared-model deduplication, and complete long-audio window coverage.

The exact pinned model archives were also downloaded and exercised locally with Sherpa-ONNX 1.13.8 on Windows CPU. All nine languages generated nonempty, finite, nonzero neural audio: Supertonic at 44,100 Hz, Piper Persian/Urdu at 22,050 Hz, and Kokoro Chinese at 24,000 Hz. One resident Whisper small INT8 model transcribed all nine generated samples and reported the requested language codes. Short Persian, Urdu, and Korean synthetic samples had word errors; this smoke check verifies routing and engine compatibility, not native-speaker pronunciation or recognition accuracy. Samsung S24 microphone quality, speed, heat, and voice quality require device testing.
